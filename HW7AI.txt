# AI Usage Disclosure

**1. Tool Used:**
Google Gemini (School/University-provided access)

**2. What It Was Used For:**
Formatting refinement, grammar/polish check, and converting raw notes into a structured Markdown template for the Team Charter assignment.

**3. The Prompt(s) Used:**
- "i wrote this as my charter for school project [pasted raw text]"
- "is there any grammer mistakes"
- "and what can be done for the formating"

**4. What Was Kept vs. What Was Changed:**
- **Kept:** The core scope, team roles, rules, success metrics, and goals that I originally wrote myself.
- **Changed:** Adopted clean Markdown headings, bullet points, and a structured team roster table layout to improve readability, while keeping my original phrasing intact.
